# Format: linkedin

**Goal:** a professional-but-human post that earns the "…see more" expand and starts a conversation. ~150–300 words. → `linkedin.md`

## Shape

1. **First line is everything** — it's the only thing shown before the fold. Make it a hook: a result, a sharp opinion, or a small confession. No "I'm excited to share…".
2. **One whitespace-separated thought per 1–2 lines** — LinkedIn rewards short, scannable lines, not paragraphs.
3. **The middle** — the insight from `subject.md`, told as a brief story or a short numbered list of lessons.
4. **Close with a question** to the audience (drives comments) or a soft link.

## Writing rules

- 3–5 hashtags at the very end, broader/professional (`#softwareengineering`, `#ai`), not the niche blog tags.
- Warm, reflective, credible tone — more "here's what I learned" than "here's my hot take". This is the professional-network channel.
- Links in comments or at the end (in-body links can suppress reach); if linking in body, keep it one clean URL.
- No emoji spray; one or two, at most, and only if they aid scanning.

## Skeleton

```
{hook first line}

{2–4 short lines: the story / the lesson}

{1–3 takeaways, optionally numbered}

{question to audience or soft CTA + link}

#tag #tag #tag
```
