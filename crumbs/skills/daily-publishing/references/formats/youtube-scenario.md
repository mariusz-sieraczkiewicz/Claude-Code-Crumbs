# Format: youtube-scenario

**Goal:** a light outline that gives you reference points for a ≤10-minute video — the main beats to hit, not a shot-by-shot script. You improvise the details on camera. → `youtube-scenario.md`

## What to produce

- **Logline** — one sentence: who it's for + what they'll be able to do after.
- **Title + thumbnail idea** — one line each.
- **Main points** — a short ordered list (roughly 4–7 bullets) covering: the hook, the 2–4 core ideas from `subject.md`, and the closing/CTA. One line per point — just enough to remember what to cover and in what order.

## Rules

- Keep it to reference points, not instructions. No per-second timestamps, no camera/B-roll directions, no full sentences to read out.
- Lead with the hook — the payoff or stakes come first.
- One idea per bullet, in the order you'd say them.

## Skeleton

```markdown
# {video title}
Logline: ...
Thumbnail: ...

## Main points
- Hook: ...
- ...
- ...
- Close / CTA: ...
```
